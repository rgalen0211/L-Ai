This page says where Ryagram's data comes from and how its films are labelled. It is for the free preview.

Where the data comes from

Ryagram films are drawn from public data.

Your project page lists what the film is made from, with the publisher of each source.

The label

Every finished film carries the label "AI-assisted (Ryagram)" in the video file's details and on the page where you collect it. Previews and contact sheets are not labelled.

Checks, and what they do not do

We run checks on every film, but the checks are not a promise.

Data can be wrong. A film can be right and still mislead if you caption it that way.

You are responsible for what you use a film for.

Not yet in the preview

Some things are not yet in the preview: uploading your own data and publishing a film page. This page will say more when they open.

You can connect an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT to your account. It acts as you. It cannot spend your credits, start a full render, publish or delete your account. Your chat with it is handled by its company, under its own terms.
