Privacy when you connect your AI

This page is for people who connect an AI assistant (Claude or ChatGPT) to their Ryagram account. It adds to the Privacy Policy; it does not replace it. [LAWYER: how this page and the policy fit together]

What a connected AI can see and do

  • It can see your account’s projects, film stories, credit balance and the state of your jobs.
  • It can create a project, save a story that Ryagram’s rules accept, and ask for a free contact sheet or a free preview. It can get a link to a finished film.
  • It cannot spend your credits and cannot start a full render. Those stay with you, inside Ryagram.
  • It cannot change your email, your password or your settings, and it cannot delete anything for you.

What Ryagram keeps about a connection

  • The name the connection goes by and its return address, when it was made and when it was last used, and which permissions you allowed.
  • A short log of each request the AI makes: the time, the name of the tool, whether it worked, a short code, and which connection and account it was. It does not keep what the AI asked for, the contents of your stories, your sign-in tokens or your email address in that log.
  • Ryagram does not read your conversation with the AI. It sees only what the AI sends to Ryagram’s tools.
  • Payment details and health information are not handled by the connection.

How long

  • A connection’s access lasts one hour at a time and is renewed in the background; a connection that is not used for 30 days ends by itself.
  • The request log is kept for 30 days. When you delete your account, your connections and permissions are deleted with it, and the log keeps no account number for you.

Your controls

In Ryagram, open Account, then Connected AIs. You see what each connection may do and when it was last used, and Disconnect ends it at once.

Who else is involved

  • The AI company you chose (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for ChatGPT) handles your conversation under its own terms and privacy policy. Ryagram does not control that. [LAWYER: wording, and whether to link their policies]
