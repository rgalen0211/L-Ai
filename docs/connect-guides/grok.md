# Grok

1. grok.com, Connectors (grok.com/connectors), New connector, Custom. URL: `<CONNECT_URL>`.
2. Complete the sign-in Grok opens: Ryagram sign-in, check the address, Allow.
3. Ask "show my Ryagram credits".

Business and Enterprise: a team admin must provision the connector first.

Known unknowns: xAI does not document OAuth or dynamic registration for custom connectors, nor its callback URL. Ryan captures the callback on the first attempt. If Grok cannot complete the sign-in, Grok is not supported yet.
