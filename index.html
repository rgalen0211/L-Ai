FAQ

Practical questions first.

Do I need to know what should be automated?

No. The best starting point is usually simply showing a repetitive computer-based task that consumes time. The diagnostic is used to decide whether automation is actually appropriate.

Is this a large-group AI training seminar?

No. L'Ai is intentionally hands-on and focused. Most engagements are one-on-one or with a very small number of people who actually perform the workflow.

What AI platform do you use?

L'Ai is currently ChatGPT-centered because that is where the strongest hands-on implementation experience sits. Other tools may be used when appropriate, but we do not pretend to be platform-neutral experts in systems we have not worked with deeply.

Will AI replace the employee?

The goal is to remove repetitive computer work from the employee's path, not to automate judgment that should remain human. In many cases the value is recovered capacity rather than eliminating a role.
