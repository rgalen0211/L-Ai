This policy describes how L'Ai uses information submitted through uselai.com and the services involved in our website and Workflow Audit.

Information you provide

When you send an inquiry or request a Workflow Audit, you provide your name, email address, and the details you choose to share about your work. Optional details may include your company, the number of people involved, approximate weekly hours, and systems or software used.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, review the task, provide the requested audit, and follow up about any implementation opportunity you discuss with us. Please do not submit passwords or sensitive customer information.

Ryagram waitlist

If you join the Ryagram waitlist, you provide your email address and, if you choose, a note about what you would make with Ryagram. We use this to contact you about Ryagram, including invitations to try it, and to understand what people want to make. It is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy. To be removed from the waitlist, email us at the address below.

Ryagram accounts

Ryagram is operated by L'Ai, LLC. This section and the ones after it cover your Ryagram account and the creator beta. The rest of this policy covers our website forms and scheduling.

Your account. To sign in we keep your email address and a protected form of your password (we never see or store the password itself), and your sign-in session is kept in your browser so you stay signed in until you sign out. We also keep your account settings. We keep a record of your credits. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, Stripe: we never see or store card details. While Ryagram is in beta, purchases run in Stripe's test mode and no real money moves.

Creator beta applications

When you apply to the creator beta you give your name, your email address, what kind of creator you are, what you make, a link to your channel or site, and the question you would answer with data. We use them to decide on invitations and to contact you about the beta. To limit spam we make a scrambled value from your network address and the date, using a secret key that we keep apart from the applications. We keep that value with your application: it changes every day, nobody who only has the applications can turn it back into your address, and we never store the address itself. Applications and that value are visible only to Ryagram's operator. If we invite you directly, without an application, we keep the invite code (with the name we recorded for it) and, once you use it, the email address you signed up with, until 12 months after you used it (or, if you never did, 12 months after it expired), and then delete them. Your films

For each film we keep what you ask for (your question, and the film's story and headline), the jobs we run and how they ended, and the files they make: contact sheets, previews, finished films, thumbnails and a receipt for each. If you ask for a film through the creator beta, we also keep your request (your question and any notes) and its status. These are private to your account: other users cannot see them. We keep them until you delete your account. A film is public only if you choose to publish its page, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Your own data

Placeholder wording for invited testers; the final wording is agreed before public launch. If you use “Your own data”, you upload a spreadsheet (.csv, .tsv, .xlsx or .ods, up to 10 MB). We keep the file in private storage, with its column names and sample values, the meaning you confirm for each column, a checksum of the file and its row count. Only you can see them; our rendering worker reads the file to make your film and for nothing else. If you choose “Store my data” we keep it until you delete it or delete your account. If you choose “Don’t keep” we remove it when your final film is complete, after 7 days without activity, or 1 day after a file could not be read. When you delete a file we remove it and keep only an id, the checksum and the row count. The figures in your films are the ones you supplied; Ryagram does not check them. Please do not upload other people’s personal information.

Who can see your data

You. The computer that draws your films (our "worker") reads only what a job needs while it runs. Ryagram's operator has administrative access to the database and storage that hold your account, as needed to run and secure the service; no screen in the app shows you or us the contents of your films, and the screens we use to understand demand show counts. Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores the data on our behalf (see its privacy policy). Stripe handles card payments (see its privacy policy). The emails we send you, such as sign-in and invitation emails, are sent through Resend (see its privacy policy).

Help requests and reports

If you use the help form we keep your email address, the topic, your message and, if you came from a film, the state and error code of that film's job (never its contents). If you report a film we keep your email address, your reason, the page, a scrambled form of your network address (made with a secret key kept apart from the reports, and used only when your address reaches us; otherwise your email address serves in its place), and the details you give; only the first five reports from one reporter in an hour keep their details, and later ones keep only the time, reporter, page and reason. Page visits

Visits to the Ryagram page are counted in a tally by day and by the link they came from. The tally uses no cookies and nothing in it identifies you; it is skipped when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

How you found us, and where people get stuck

When you open the Ryagram page we note, in our own database, that a visit happened and where the link came from: the tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) and the website that sent you, for example youtube.com. If your visit came from such a link, we keep that source in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) until you sign in; then we attach it to your account and delete it from your browser. After you sign up we record against your account which steps you reach (signing up, your first project, a contact sheet, a preview, a film request and its delivery, buying credits, starting or cancelling a plan, and coming back after 7 and 30 days), so we can see where people get stuck. We store nothing else about you for this: no address, no device or browser details, no cookies and no third-party trackers. Only Ryagram's operator can see it. These records are deleted 13 months after they happen; if you delete your account, your source is deleted and your records are no longer linked to you. None of this runs when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Deleting your account

Under Your account you can delete your account: type your email to confirm. Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. We remove your files and then your login, which deletes everything linked to it. This cannot be undone. We cannot delete while a film is being drawn: wait or cancel it. Our hosting plan keeps daily backups for 7 days, so something you deleted may remain in those backups for up to 7 days and is then removed.

Forms and scheduling

Our forms send your submission to Formspree for handling. Formspree processes submitted information and technical information associated with the request. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.

If you choose to schedule a call, the scheduling link opens Google Calendar. Google processes the booking details you provide, and L'Ai receives the appointment information needed for the call. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Website services

Our pages load the L'Ai typeface through Google Fonts, which involves a request from your browser to Google. Ryagram films on our site are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode. Each film’s thumbnail loads from YouTube’s servers when the page opens; the player itself loads only when you press play, and Google may collect information about your viewing then. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Website and service providers may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver their services.

Optional analytics and advertising measurement

Meta Pixel is currently not active on this website. If configured and activated, it can measure page visits, views of the Workflow Audit page, successful audit submissions, and clicks on the scheduling button to help assess advertising performance. Meta may use cookies and browser or device information and associate activity with information it holds. Our event code does not send the text of your task, your name, or your email address as event parameters. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics 4 is not currently configured. If enabled in the future, it would be used to understand website visits and usage. We will update this notice to reflect the active configuration.

We count visits to the Ryagram page with a simple tally of page loads by day and link source: cookieless, with no personal profiles and no ad tracking. The tally stores no IP address or visitor identifier.

Submitting an audit request does not itself activate analytics or advertising measurement. Browser settings can limit cookies; those settings may also affect third-party services.

Questions and requests

Contact ryagram@uselai.com with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information you have provided. We will review your request and respond, including any limits that apply.
