Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026 · Effective September 25, 2026

This policy covers L'Ai engagements (a single Workflow Sprint or diagnostic-scoped project). It works alongside our Terms of Service; where the two conflict for a billing question, this policy governs.

Cancelling before work begins

Any deposit is fully refundable if you cancel before work begins. Email ryan.galen@uselai.com to cancel and request a refund.

Cancelling after work begins

Once work has begun, fees for work already performed — including diagnostic time, discovery, and any delivered portion of the workflow — are non-refundable. If a diagnostic determines a workflow is not worth pursuing before implementation begins, any implementation fee paid in advance for that same engagement is refunded in full.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through our payment processor, typically within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Disputes and chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please email us first at ryan.galen@uselai.com so we can resolve it directly — most issues can be sorted out faster this way than through a bank dispute.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision. Changes apply to engagements entered into after the update; they don't retroactively change the terms of an engagement already underway.
