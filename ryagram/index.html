How it works

  1. 1

    Ask

    Tell us the question you want data to answer.

  2. 2

    We draw it

    Ryagram builds an animated film from public data, with every source listed.

  3. 3

    You get it, checked

    Each film is checked against its data first, then it is yours to publish.

Every film is checked against its data before you get it: honest axes, every number traced, uncertainty stated.
