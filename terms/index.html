1. What Ryagram is

Ryagram turns public data into short animated films. It is a free preview. It changes often, and we do not promise it will always be available.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. To create an account you confirm that you are. Sign-up is open to anyone who is 18 or older.

3. Your account

  • Give a real email address. Keep your password to yourself.
  • One account is for one person. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • You can delete your account yourself (see section 9).
  • We can close an account that breaks these terms or puts the service or other people at risk.

4. What you send us

You can upload a spreadsheet (.csv, .tsv, .xlsx or .ods, up to 10 MB). Only upload data you have the right to use, and do not upload other people's personal information.

We keep your file in private storage and use it to make your films. When you upload, you choose what happens to it:

  • Store my data: we keep it until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Don't keep my data: we delete it once your final film is made, after 7 days without activity, or 1 day after we could not read it. An upload that was started and never finished is deleted after 1 day.
  • When you delete a file: it waits 7 days under "Recently deleted", so you can restore it. Then it is removed. You can also remove it at once with "Delete forever now".

5. Your films

A film is private to your account unless you choose to publish its page. If you publish it, anyone with the link can see it. You can stop sharing at any time. Copies other people already took are not taken back.

A film you made is kept for 30 days after it is delivered. After that you can no longer open it. Its file is removed within a minute or so, and we try again every minute until it is gone. A download link works for one hour. A link made before the 30 days ended can keep working until its hour is up.

If you delete an upload, the films, previews and contact sheets made from it are removed by our hourly clean-up. This includes a finished film. If your upload went because you chose "Don't keep" (or it could not be read), a finished film you took delivery of stays until its own 30 days end. Its previews and contact sheets go.

6. Public data and AI

Films are drawn from public data. Data can be wrong. We run checks on every film, but the checks are not a promise. A film can be right and still mislead if you caption it that way. You are responsible for what you publish.

Every film carries the label "AI-assisted (Ryagram)".

7. Connecting your own AI

You can connect an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT to your account. This is open to every account, except accounts used to run Ryagram itself. The AI acts as you, and you are responsible for what it does with your account. It cannot start a full render, spend your credits, change your account or delete things for you. You can disconnect it at any time. Your AI provider has its own terms.

8. What you must not do

Do not use Ryagram to break the law, to attack, overload or probe it, to get around its limits or checks, to resell it, or to upload content you have no right to use.

To report a public film (child safety, private information, harassment or copyright), use the Report page. For those reports we usually hide the film at once while we review it. We reply to your email within 7 days.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account under Your account. You type your email to confirm. We cannot delete it while a film is being made: wait, or cancel the film first.

We remove your films and uploaded files, replace your email in support requests and invite records, and then delete your login. If your account has any credit history, we keep that record, we block your login at once, and an administrator closes the account by hand within 30 days.

The Privacy Policy says what stays after you delete.
